Assay is a covenant operations workspace for synthetic credit data. It turns a maximum leverage agreement into a reviewable decision trail: source-linked terms, deterministic arithmetic, a compliance proof, and a manager decision.
The analyst starts at the facility. One encrypted agreement, a page-linked review trail, and a redaction step that must be confirmed before any external model sees the text.
Extraction returns one covenant with its threshold, definitions, permitted add-backs and verbatim evidence. Every extraction is bound to fresh Intel TDX and NVIDIA NRAS attestation, a request nonce and a signed provider response. The analyst confirms the terms. Attested financial inputs are converted to integer minor units and sealed. Decimal arithmetic produces the baseline and two stress scenarios, an independent verifier recomputes the predicate, and a fixed circuit proves the baseline inequality.
The manager sees a deliberately restricted view: threshold, proof status and scenario bands, but no raw amounts or named counterparties. Both stress cases breach, so approval requires an explicit risk acknowledgement and a written rationale.
The auditor verifies the per-borrower signed hash chain and sees the approval evidence, including the exact proof and analysis version.
Provider status stays honest: NEAR AI attested, Kite testnet provider-blocked, Reppo disabled. Assay records evidence, not a promise.
